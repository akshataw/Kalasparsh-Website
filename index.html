04 Aftercare

Heal it right

A great tattoo is half artistry, half aftercare. Follow these steps and your ink stays sharp for years.

  1. First 2–4 hours

    Keep it wrapped

    Leave the protective film on as advised. Then wash gently with lukewarm water and fragrance-free soap, and pat dry with a clean paper towel.

  2. Days 1–7

    Clean & moisturise

    Wash twice a day and apply a thin layer of the recommended aftercare balm. Thin is key — your skin needs to breathe.

  3. Weeks 2–4

    Let it peel

    Flaking and itching are normal. Don’t scratch or pick at it — tap it lightly instead and keep moisturising.

  4. For life

    Protect the ink

    Once healed, use SPF 50 sunscreen on your tattoo. Sun exposure is the number one reason tattoos fade.

Do

  • Wash your hands before touching the tattoo
  • Wear loose, clean clothing over it
  • Drink plenty of water and rest well
  • Message us if anything looks or feels off

Don’t

  • Swim in pools, the sea or hot tubs for 2–3 weeks
  • Soak it in baths, saunas or steam rooms
  • Expose it to direct sun or tanning beds
  • Pick, scratch or re-wrap it in cling film

07 FAQ

Good questions

Everything people usually ask before their first (or fifth) tattoo.

How do I book, and do I need to pay a deposit?

Send the booking form above, or message us on WhatsApp or Instagram with your idea, placement and size. Once we agree on the design and quote, a small advance (adjusted in the final price) confirms your slot.

How much does a tattoo cost?

Price depends on size, detail and placement. Small pieces have a fixed studio minimum and larger work is quoted per session. You always get a clear quote before we start — no surprises.

Does it hurt? Can I use numbing cream?

Everyone feels it differently; most people describe it as a strong scratch. Bony areas like ribs, ankles and spine are more sensitive. Talk to us before using any numbing product so it doesn’t affect your skin.

How long does a session take?

Small pieces take 1–2 hours including stencil and setup. Larger pieces are split into sessions of 3–5 hours, with breaks whenever you need them.

Can you create a custom design or Marathi / Devanagari script?

Yes — every design is drawn from scratch for you. Omkar specialises in Marathi and Devanagari lettering, so names, shlokas and quotes are laid out with correct spelling and beautiful flow.

Is there a minimum age? Do I need ID?

You must be 18 or older and carry a valid photo ID. We don’t tattoo minors, even with parental consent.

How should I prepare for my session?

Sleep well, eat a proper meal, stay hydrated and skip alcohol for 24 hours before. Wear comfortable clothes that give easy access to the area being tattooed.

Do you do cover-ups and touch-ups?

Yes. Send a clear photo of the existing tattoo for a cover-up consultation. Touch-ups on our own work are free within 3 months, once the tattoo has fully healed.
